Oracle Eloqua is a marketing automation platform that provides tools for lead management, email marketing, and marketing campaign management through comprehensive REST APIs. It enables marketing teams to create, execute, and measure the effectiveness of marketing programs and campaigns.

**Areas:** CRM · Email Marketing · Lead Management · Marketing Automation · Campaign Management · Bulk Data · Landing Pages · Forms · Reporting · B2B Marketing

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Oracle Eloqua: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
